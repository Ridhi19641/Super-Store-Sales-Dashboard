
Super Store Sales Dashboard & Sales Forecasting (Power BI)
📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Super Store Sales Dashboard and 15-Day Sales Forecasting Dashboard developed in Power BI. The dashboard analyzes sales performance across regions, customer segments, product categories, payment modes, and states, while also providing short-term sales forecasts to support data-driven decision-making.

The project demonstrates skills in:

Data Cleaning & Transformation
Data Modeling
DAX Measures
Interactive Dashboard Design
Time Series Forecasting
Business Intelligence Reporting

🎯 Objectives
Analyze overall sales, profit, and order quantity.
Identify top-performing regions, states, and product categories.
Understand customer purchasing behavior across segments and payment methods.
Evaluate shipping mode performance.
Visualize geographical sales distribution.
Forecast future sales trends for business planning.

📊 Dashboard Features

1. Sales Performance Dashboard
Regional Sales Analysis
Customer Segment Distribution
Payment Method Analysis
Total Sales, Quantity, Profit & Ship Days KPIs
Monthly Sales Trend Analysis
Monthly Profit Trend Analysis
Sales by Shipping Mode
Product Category Performance
Product Sub-Category Analysis
State-wise Sales & Profit Map
Region Slicer (Central, East, South, West)

2. Sales Forecast Dashboard
Historical Sales Trend Analysis
15-Day Sales Forecast
Forecast Confidence Interval
Top Performing States by Sales
Interactive Date Filtering

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Power BI Desktop
Power Query
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

📈 Key Insights
West region generated the highest share of sales.
Consumer segment contributed the largest portion of revenue.
Cash on Delivery (COD) emerged as the most preferred payment method.
Office Supplies was the top-performing product category.
California recorded the highest sales among all states.
Forecasting analysis indicates continued sales growth with seasonal fluctuations.


Super Store Sales Dashboard & Sales Forecasting (Power BI)
📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Super Store Sales Dashboard and 15-Day Sales Forecasting Dashboard developed in Power BI. The dashboard analyzes sales performance across regions, customer segments, product categories, payment modes, and states, while also providing short-term sales forecasts to support data-driven decision-making.

The project demonstrates skills in:

Data Cleaning & Transformation
Data Modeling
DAX Measures
Interactive Dashboard Design
Time Series Forecasting
Business Intelligence Reporting

🎯 Objectives
Analyze overall sales, profit, and order quantity.
Identify top-performing regions, states, and product categories.
Understand customer purchasing behavior across segments and payment methods.
Evaluate shipping mode performance.
Visualize geographical sales distribution.
Forecast future sales trends for business planning.

🔍 Business Questions Addressed

This dashboard helps answer the following key business questions:

Which regions contribute the highest sales and profit, and where should the business focus its growth efforts?
Which customer segments generate the most revenue and drive overall business performance?
Which product categories and sub-categories are the primary revenue drivers?
Which states and geographic markets offer the strongest sales opportunities?
What future sales trends can be expected based on historical data, and how can they support inventory and resource planning?

📈 Key Business Insights
1)Sales Performance
The West Region contributes the largest share of total sales, making it the strongest revenue-generating region.
The Consumer Segment accounts for nearly half of overall sales, indicating that retail customers are the primary revenue drivers.
Cash on Delivery (COD) is the most frequently used payment method, highlighting customer preference for flexible payment options.

2)Product Analysis
Office Supplies generates the highest sales among product categories.
Phones, Chairs, and Binders are the leading product sub-categories, contributing significantly to overall revenue.

3)Geographic Analysis
California emerges as the highest-performing state in terms of sales.
Several high-performing states indicate strong regional demand and potential expansion opportunities.

4)Logistics & Shipping
Standard Class Shipping contributes the largest portion of sales, suggesting customers prioritize cost-effective delivery options over faster shipping methods.

5)Forecasting Insights
Sales forecasting predicts stable demand patterns over the next 15 days.
Forecast confidence intervals provide a range of expected sales performance, supporting proactive inventory and workforce planning.
